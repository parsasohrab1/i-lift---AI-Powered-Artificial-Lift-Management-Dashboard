## Title
Conformal Alarm Thresholds for Oil-Well Fault Detection on Real Petrobras 3W Data: Detection Rate versus Warning Time

## Abstract (draft)
Fault detection in oil wells must keep false alarms low while warning early enough to act. We study conformal alarm thresholds as an add-on to an artificial-lift management system, using the public Petrobras 3W data (82 normal and 32 real fault instances). The conformal threshold kept the false-alarm rate at 0.12 for a target of 0.10. Faults were detected in 75% to 100% of the instances, depending on the fault type, but a warning before the start of the event was given in only 25% to 47% of them. The study therefore shows that a controlled false-alarm rate is attainable on real well data, while early warning remains the limiting factor. More 3W instances, a comparison with Isolation Forest and CUSUM, ROC and delay curves, and the identification of the product's filed claims (claim 1566 on drilling anomaly detection is adjacent) are [to be measured]. Conformal alarms of this kind have been published before, so the novelty of the study is low.
